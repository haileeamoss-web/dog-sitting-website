Policies

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled service will receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled service are non-refundable.

No-Show Policy

If a client is unavailable or fails to provide access at the scheduled service time, the booking will be considered a no-show and is non-refundable.

Refund Policy

Refunds are issued only for cancellations that meet the cancellation policy requirements. Once a service has started, refunds will not be provided.
